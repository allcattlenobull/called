# Research and trust

The public research audit is available at the site’s Research and trust page. It maps each major Called component to the evidence it can honestly claim, the evidence it does not yet have, and the higher-evidence replacement or validation step.

The standard is intentionally strict:

- A paper must match the construct, population, administration, scoring method, and intended outcome.
- Evidence for a validated instrument does not validate Called’s custom item bank.
- Reliability, construct validity, criterion validity, fairness, and intended-use limits must be considered separately.
- Product efficacy requires evaluation of the Called workflow itself; it cannot be borrowed from papers about adjacent methods.

As of this audit, Called treats custom aptitude puzzles as practice only and no longer uses their result in career ranking. Simulation response keys remain learning feedback, not a validated score or skill signal. Interests, values, conversations, experiments, reality checks, labor-market data, and transparent matching remain exploratory evidence with explicit limits.

This is a focused audit, not an exhaustive systematic review. The page links every cited source and records the research method and relevance for the component where it is used.
